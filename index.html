Timeless Customs

Odia Wedding Traditions

Nirbandha

The solemn vow and formal alliance, sealed with the auspicious offering of betel nuts and coconuts to the divine.

Baran & Aarti

A royal welcome for the groom, illuminated by the sacred Aarti flame, welcoming him to the threshold of a new life.

Hastaganthi

The spiritual intertwining of our hands, draped in ceremonial cloth and blessed by ancient Vedic hymns.

Saptapadi

Seven sacred steps around the holy fire, each a lifelong vow of friendship, nourishment, and unwavering devotion.

Sala Bidha

A cherished, lighthearted ritual where the brother-in-law's gentle tap symbolizes affectionate family bonds and sweet camaraderie.

Gruhaprabesa

The bride's auspicious first steps into her new home, gently overturning a rice pot to usher in an era of boundless prosperity.
