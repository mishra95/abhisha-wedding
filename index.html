Cultural Heritage

Odia Wedding Traditions

Nirbandha

The sacred formal engagement where families exchange holy vows and betel nuts (Gua), seeking divine blessings.

Baraat (Baran)

The joyous arrival of the groom and his family, welcomed at the entrance with a traditional aarti by the bride's mother.

Hastaganthi

The knotting of hands over sacred grass and betel leaves, symbolizing an inseparable spiritual bond of love.

Saptapadi

The seven sacred steps around the holy fire, solemnizing lifelong companionship, health, fidelity, and happiness.

Sala Bidha

A fun, playful Odia tradition where the bride's brother playfully taps the groom's back before gifts are exchanged.

Griha Pravesh

The auspicious welcoming of the new bride into the groom's home with an overturned pot of rice symbolizing abundance.
