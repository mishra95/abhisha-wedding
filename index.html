Sacred Heritage

Odia Wedding Traditions

Nirbandha

The commitment ceremony where families exchange auspicious betel nuts (Gua) and coconuts, officially formalizing the wedding alliance.

Baran & Aarti

The grand welcome of the groom. The bride's mother performs aarti, blessing him before entering the sacred mandap.

Hastaganthi

The holy knotting of the couple's hands draped in cloth with betel leaves and mango twigs, blessed by sacred chants.

Saptapadi

Seven sacred circumambulations around the sacred fire (Agni Devata), symbolizing eternal nourishment, strength, and companionship.

Sala Bidha

An affectionate and lighthearted tradition where the brother-in-law playfully gives a gentle punch on the groom's back before gifts are exchanged.

Bahugora / Griha Pravesh

The grand entrance of the new bride into the groom's household, gently overturning a measuring pot of rice symbolizing prosperity.
